# DECISIONS.md

## 1. Why this approach over the obvious alternative?

I chose to build the submission around Courage National Talent Search (CNTS), an existing product, rather than creating a fictional landing page or a disconnected static mockup. This allowed the homepage to represent a real product flow instead of only demonstrating visual design.

The implementation uses the existing Next.js/React component structure so that the homepage remains connected to the actual product architecture and can evolve with the application. I prioritized a clear value proposition, strong visual hierarchy, responsive behavior, and a product-focused presentation over adding unnecessary decorative elements.

The main design decision was to make the product itself visible through the talent-profile experience rather than relying only on marketing claims. This gives the visitor an immediate understanding of what CNTS actually provides.

## 2. One trade-off made under the time limit

The primary trade-off was between breadth of functionality and refinement. Instead of implementing numerous secondary interactions, I prioritized the core homepage experience, responsive layout, visual consistency, and the primary product interaction.

With a full week, I would spend additional time on deeper accessibility testing, performance profiling, more device-specific responsive testing, and further refinement of micro-interactions and edge cases.

## 3. Where did you use AI tools, and what did you personally verify or change afterward?

AI tools were used as development assistance during implementation, including exploring implementation approaches, generating or refining code, debugging issues, and considering UI alternatives.

I did not treat AI output as automatically correct. I personally reviewed and modified the generated implementation, verified the resulting behavior in the browser, checked responsive layouts, tested interactions and navigation, and resolved implementation issues that appeared during development.

The final architecture, product decisions, visual direction, and shipped implementation were reviewed by me, and I made the final decisions about what was included in the submission.
